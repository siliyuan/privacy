ModForge Privacy Policy (Last updated: 2026-10-23)

What we collect
1. Account data: username, email address and password (stored only as a hash), plus an optional avatar.
2. Content you create: board data and preview images for cloud sync, and the text or canvas images you submit to the AI drawing feature.
3. Purchase records: the result of membership purchases made through Google Play.
4. Access logs: IP address, request time and endpoint, used for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it
Only to provide sign-in, cloud board storage, AI drawing, membership entitlements and basic security. We do not run ads and we do not sell data.

Third-party services
- Google Play Billing: processes membership payments.
- A large language model provider (DeepSeek): processes AI drawing requests; only the text and images required for that request are sent.

Retention and deletion
Account and board data are kept on our servers until you delete your account. You can permanently delete your account and all cloud data in the app under Me → Delete account, or by emailing 1260608576@qq.com.

Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.
