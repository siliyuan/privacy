## 中文版

### 1. 引言

本隐私权政策说明 ChargingSound（以下简称"本应用"）在你使用本应用时如何处理相关信息。
我们非常重视你的隐私，本应用**无需注册账号**，也不会要求你提供姓名、手机号、邮箱等个人身份信息。
使用本应用即表示你已阅读并同意本政策。

### 2. 我们收集与使用的信息

**2.1 我们不收集的信息**
本应用不收集、不存储、不上传你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、通讯录、短信、精确位置、照片、摄像头或麦克风内容。

**2.2 在本机处理、不上传的信息**
- **电池与设备状态**：为显示电量、电池健康度、温度等信息，本应用在本机读取系统电池状态。这些数据仅在本机展示，不会上传到服务器。
- **你选择的音频文件**：当你选择自定义充电音效时，本应用会在本机读取该音频文件用于播放，不会上传或分享。
- **应用设置**：音效选择、开关等偏好仅保存在你的设备本地（SharedPreferences）。

**2.3 由第三方服务收集的信息**
为提供广告与内购功能，本应用集成了以下 Google 服务，它们可能会收集相应信息：

| 服务 | 用途 | 可能收集的信息 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google AdMob（广告） | 展示横幅广告与插屏广告 | 广告标识符（Android Advertising ID）、设备型号与系统版本、粗略地理位置（由 IP 推断）、广告互动数据 |
| Google Play 结算 | 处理音效购买 | 交易标识与购买凭证（**我们不接触、不存储你的支付卡信息**） |
| Firebase Analytics | 统计应用使用情况以改进产品 | 应用使用事件、设备型号与系统版本、应用版本等匿名统计信息 |
| Firebase Storage | 提供可下载的音效文件 | 仅用于下载音效文件，不涉及你的个人信息 |

上述第三方服务的隐私政策：
- Google 隐私权政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google 广告与隐私：https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- Firebase 隐私与安全：https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 3. 权限说明

| 权限 | 用途 |
| --- | --- |
| `INTERNET` | 加载广告、下载音效、连接 Google 服务 |
| `com.android.vending.BILLING` | 完成音效的 Google Play 内购 |
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | 读取你选择的自定义音频文件 |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` | 在后台持续监听充电状态并播放音效 |
| 广告标识符（AD_ID） | 由广告 SDK 使用，用于展示广告 |

所有权限均仅用于实现上述对应功能。你可以随时在系统设置中查看并管理这些权限。

### 4. 信息的共享

我们不会出售你的个人信息。仅在以下情形下信息会被共享：
- 与上述 Google 服务提供商共享，用于实现广告与内购功能；
- 依法律法规、司法或行政机关的强制要求而披露。

### 5. 数据存储与安全

本应用不设有用于存储你个人信息的服务器，你的偏好设置与选中的音效均保存在本机。
第三方 SDK 收集的数据由其自行存储与保护，请参阅其隐私政策。

### 6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下（或你所在司法辖区规定的相应年龄）的儿童。
我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如你认为我们无意中收集了此类信息，请联系我们予以删除。

### 7. 你的权利与选择

- **关闭个性化广告**：可在设备的「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」中重置或删除广告标识符，并关闭广告个性化。
- **撤回数据收集同意**：卸载本应用即可停止全部数据收集。
- **管理权限**：可在系统设置中随时关闭本应用的各项权限（关闭后相关功能将不可用）。

### 8. 本政策的变更

我们可能会适时更新本政策。更新后我们会在本页面公布最新版本并更新"生效日期"。
重大变更将通过应用内或商店说明提示你。

### 9. 联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问、意见或投诉，请联系：siliyuan@outlook.com

## English Version

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ChargingSound (the "App") handles information when you use it.
We take your privacy seriously. The App **requires no account registration** and does not ask you for
personally identifying information such as your name, phone number, or email address.
By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Policy.

### 2. Information We Collect and Use

**2.1 Information We Do Not Collect**
The App does not collect, store, or upload your name, phone number, email, contacts, messages, precise
location, photos, camera, or microphone content.

**2.2 Information Processed On Your Device Only**
- **Battery and device status**: To display battery level, health, and temperature, the App reads the
  system battery status locally. This data is shown on your device only and is never uploaded.
- **Audio files you select**: When you choose a custom charging sound, the App reads that audio file
  locally for playback only. It is never uploaded or shared.
- **App settings**: Your preferences (selected sound, toggles, etc.) are stored only on your device
  (SharedPreferences).

**2.3 Information Collected by Third-Party Services**
To provide advertising and in-app purchases, the App integrates the following Google services, which
may collect the corresponding information:

| Service | Purpose | Information that may be collected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google AdMob (ads) | Display banner and interstitial ads | Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), device model and OS version, coarse location (inferred from IP), ad interaction data |
| Google Play Billing | Process sound effect purchases | Transaction identifiers and purchase tokens (**we never access or store your payment card details**) |
| Firebase Analytics | Measure app usage to improve the product | App usage events, device model and OS version, app version, and other anonymous statistics |
| Firebase Storage | Deliver downloadable sound files | Used only to download sound files; no personal information is involved |

Privacy policies of these third-party services:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Ads & Privacy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- Firebase Privacy & Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 3. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `INTERNET` | Load ads, download sound files, connect to Google services |
| `com.android.vending.BILLING` | Complete in-app purchases via Google Play |
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Read the custom audio files you select |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` | Keep monitoring charging status and playing sounds in the background |
| Advertising ID (AD_ID) | Used by the ads SDK to serve advertising |

Each permission is used solely for the function described above. You can review and manage these
permissions at any time in your device settings.

### 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only:
- with the Google service providers listed above, to enable advertising and in-app purchases; or
- when required to disclose by law, court order, or a competent authority.

### 5. Data Storage and Security

The App operates no server for storing your personal information; your preferences and selected sounds
are kept on your device. Data collected by third-party SDKs is stored and protected by those providers;
please refer to their privacy policies.

### 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction).
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently
done so, please contact us so we can delete it.

### 7. Your Rights and Choices

- **Opt out of personalized ads**: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and disable ad
  personalization in your device's "Settings → Privacy → Ads".
- **Withdraw consent**: Uninstalling the App stops all data collection.
- **Manage permissions**: You can revoke any permission at any time in your device settings
  (the related feature will then be unavailable).

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an
updated "Effective Date". Material changes will be communicated in-app or via the store listing.

### 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or complaints about this Policy, please contact: siliyuan@outlook.com
